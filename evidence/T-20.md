`./verify.ps1` passed. It started only the database service in a newly named
isolated project and found **zero tables in public/library**. The image contains

for the private local CA; it does not disable certificate/hostname checks.

on 127.0.0.1:8443. Local API/browser journeys and the trusted-CA Node benchmark
also passed; their records belong to app/tool evidence.
